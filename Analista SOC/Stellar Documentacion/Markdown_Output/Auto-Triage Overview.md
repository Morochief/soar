Auto-Triage Overview

The automatic triage of alerts and user-reported phishing attempts is available as part of the
Early Access Program (EAP) and is only supported on Stellar Cyber in SaaS deployments.

 The automatic triage of alerts and user-reported phishing are only supported on Stellar
Cyber in SaaS deployments and require an add-on license, which you can request from
your sales representative. If you used these features through participation in the Early
Access Program, contact your sales representative to continue with an add-on license or to
request that the services be disabled.

Auto-Triage features automate the analysis of ingested data using an embedded AI-powered
triage agents and internal and integrated threat intelligence. Use the following topics to

About Deployment Availability
AI-driven features are introduced in Stellar Cyber SaaS deployments. This approach enables
rapid innovation and ensures consistent performance, scalability, and security for AI-intensive
capabilities. SaaS environments provide the controlled infrastructure, elasticity, and update
cadence required to safely operate and continuously improve these advanced features.

Stellar Cyber remains committed to supporting on-premises deployments and continues to
evaluate which AI capabilities can be extended to those environments over time, based on
technical feasibility and customer demand.
